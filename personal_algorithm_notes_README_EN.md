## About

This repository documents my personal learning process in **robotics-related algorithms and technologies**.

My study process includes following **public MIT and Stanford courses**, reading papers and books, deriving formulas by hand, downloading and running code for learning, and examining important concepts in depth. Finally, I **use AI to help organize and structure** the material, then personally review and revise the notes.

These notes are intended only to document my learning process, make future review easier, and save time otherwise spent revisiting sources, concepts, and derivations.
